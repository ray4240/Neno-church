About Our Ministry

Proclaiming the Word of Power & Deliverance

Neno Evangelism Centre was founded by Apostle Dr. James Maina Ng'ang'a with a core mission: to preach the uncompromised gospel of Jesus Christ, bring healing to the brokenhearted, and set captives free through power and deliverance.

Through Sasa TV Network, the ministry reaches millions of homes across Kenya and globally with daily live services, intercessory prayer lines, and healing crusades.
